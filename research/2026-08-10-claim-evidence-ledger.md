## Claims That Need More Evidence Before Manuscript Use

| Claim | Missing evidence |
| --- | --- |
| The result generalizes beyond Fashion-MNIST. | The CIFAR-10 spatial-feature path gives bounded harder-workload evidence under mild/moderate architecture envelopes, but broader generalization still needs a stronger target or a deliberately narrow claim. |
| The athermal-compensated MRR profile is physically achievable for the exact weight-bank design. | Replace bridge anchors with device-specific measured or primary-source values. |
| The source/converter stack can meet the target in a productizable design. | Find or design a source chain that meets rate, precision, SFDR, energy, and modulator-drive requirements together. |
| The 3-seed 10-epoch result is robust enough for a strong empirical claim. | Expand seed count or narrow the claim to a methods/architecture demonstration. |
| Recalibration policy is optimized. | Run ablations separating scheduled refresh, projection-MSE trigger, calibration sample count, drift, readout, and source coding. |

## Immediate Manuscript Consequences

- The introduction should lead with converter-aware chained inference, not
  "photonic matrix multiplication is fast."
- The methods section needs an explicit assumptions table before any results.
- The results section should separate chain viability, physical drift/noise, and
  source/converter limits.
- The discussion should treat the converter wall as a central finding.
- The limitations section must keep the hardware-demonstration boundary blunt.
